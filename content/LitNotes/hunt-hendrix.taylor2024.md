## @hunt-hendrix.taylor2024
Title: Solidarity: the past, present, and future of a world-changing idea
Year: 2024
Authors: Leah Hunt-Hendrix, Astra Taylor

### Annotations
"successfully resisting plutocratic efforts to split people apart in order to dominate public life requires creating new coalitions that have the power to influence our laws and institutions, and developing the stamina to sustain these new political forms. By conceiving ourselves as intrinsically bound in relationships of mutuality and care that span generations, we can understand our obligation to provide a secure and dignified life to others while also recognizing that we are entitled to such an existence ourselves." xvii  
  
"Solidarity means not writing anyone off completely, not throwing anyone away. It holds out hope that systems and individuals can change." xxxiv  
  
Leroux saw solidarity and dependency as key with individualism as obstacle. Cf Ayn Rand who saw within towards collective good as sameness and loss of individual. 8  
  
Creation of working class in 1800s not as automatic process, but built by formerly individual guild members coming to collective idea that they were workers in opposition to aristocracy and owners. 9  
  
Durkheim: pre-modern societies had solidarity based on sameness (I think in opposition to other, which forms ethnicity), but modern societies form solidarity on difference because we need various skills to survive, since hardly anyone is self-sufficient. Cf perils of horizontality in protest era in [[bevins2023 |  Bevins If We Burn]] 11-12  
  
Comparison of Marx and Durkheim on solidarity 13-14  
  
Marx ignored how to maintain social cohesion after revolution, in socialist and communist experiments have seen that solidarity is required to prevent backsliding into previous hierarchy or developing new worse forms 14

Forbearance is not a wiping away of responsibility. Rather, it is the recognition that everyone can be redeemed, even if some losses can never be recouped. While we want to hold ourselves and others accountable - and there should be consequences when we hurt people, cross lines, and break norms that are important to a community - we must also have measures that allow us to recover when we fail and repair when we transgress. Everyone is fallible, and everyone has the potential to be transformed." 304
