## @bevins2023
Title: If we burn: the mass protest decade and the missing revolution
Year: 2023
Authors: Vincent Bevins

### Annotations
## 20. Reconstructing the Past

- Protest isn’t enough, need to have vision for the future and a plan to get there. If things are overturned, something else will fill the void, so need to make sure it’s a replacement for the better and not worse. (259)
    
- Horizontality often leaves a void of leadership. Need organization in form of vanguard, union, etc. so someone else doesn’t take charge. (266)
    
- Contrast horizontalism with mutual aid group structure in [[spade2020 | Dean Spade’s book “Mutual Aid”]] (271)
    
- For any uprising, need to ask: “Who comprises this movement, what specifically are they asking for, and what is likely to happen if they win?” (278)
    

## 21. Building the Future

- “Organizations are effective, and representation is important.” Need to act collectively and resist “divide and conquer” tactics, since “individualism tends to re-enforce existing power structures” (283)
    
- Beware of use of protest as be all and end all solution, since at times other solutions like boycotts and strikes provide better leverage for change. (283)
