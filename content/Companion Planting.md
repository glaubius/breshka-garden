I recently attended a free workshop on Companion Planting with my spouse. The Master Gardener presented on the benefits of Companion Planting (cultivating different plants next to each other for benefits) and provided handouts with more information. She advocated against using herbicides and pesticides in yards not only because they might be taken up with food plants, but also because they decrease pollinators.

Our garden is primarily flowers, but we usually get 2 tomato plants from our CSA and grow them in pots. We usually put mulch around the tomato plants, which has probably spread fungus to the tomato plants in the past, but this year plan to put basil in the pots instead to provide cover against weeds and repel blight-spreading pests (and hopefully provide leaves for pesto!).

Companion planting can also provide a useful analogy for [[Digital Garden]].
