https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/

A static-site generator built to work with Obsidian (although it's flexible enough to work with other tools). One of the main reasons Quartz was created was to help with building digital gardens. For an easier method with less terminal window and github, try [[How to build a digital garden with TiddlyWiki]].
