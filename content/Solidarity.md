Solidarity is the act of standing with other people because we believe that we have an obligation to care for each other and fight for better lives and equality together. 

Those who are in solidarity do so, not because they are the same, but because they fight for a better world where differences are celebrated, not used as wedge issues to drive people apart. For there to be true solidarity, there need to be differences between people, because if all people in a group are the same, sharing the same values and ideas, there is no higher reason to work together. And a group of people with only similarities is necessarily small. Instead solidarity is built by working through differences in opinion, through conversation and planning. 

Solidarity is not achieved easily, but is a necessary part of building power for the left. But as of 2025, the left is very fragmented and lacking in solidarity, a problem which needs to be addressed before it's too late.
